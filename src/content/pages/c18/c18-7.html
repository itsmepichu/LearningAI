• Ownership: every AI system has a named owning team accountable for quality, cost, incidents and compliance; every component (prompts, index, fine-tuned model, gateway routes, eval suite) has an owner. Shared platforms (gateway, vector DB, observability) have platform owners with clear interfaces.
  • Release documentation: release notes per release manifest: what changed, evaluation results vs previous, known limitations, rollout plan, rollback procedure. Model cards (13.7) and system cards (intended use, risks, controls) for governance (16.8).
  • Operational readiness review: before launch or handover, verify the system can be operated: SLOs defined, dashboards and alerts live, runbooks written and tested, on-call staffed and trained, capacity plan with measured numbers (15.10), DR tested, security review done, cost budgets configured.
  • Service handover: when a project team hands a system to an operations or product team: walkthrough of architecture and traces, access granted, runbook drills, shadow on-call, and a period of joint ownership before the original team steps back.
