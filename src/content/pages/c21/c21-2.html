Intuition

Most AI bills are a long list of small, repeated waste: the same long instructions sent with every request, more retrieved text than the answer needs, and answers longer than anyone reads. Token optimisation removes the waste one item at a time.

Each cut must be checked against quality. Fewer retrieved chunks can mean wrong answers, and a wrong answer that a person fixes costs far more than the tokens saved.

Caching needs a stable prefix

Putting the date, a user name or a request id near the start of the prompt makes every request’s prefix different, so nothing is ever served from the cache. Put changing details after the stable instructions.

The numbers

Output costs most (illustrative prices: input 3, output 15 dollars per million). At 3 million requests a month:

  • Cutting answers from 500 to 300 tokens saves 200 × 3,000,000 × 15 ÷ 1,000,000 = 9,000 dollars a month.
  • Cutting 200 input tokens saves only 200 × 3,000,000 × 3 ÷ 1,000,000 = 1,800 dollars.
  • Caching 2,160 of 2,400 instruction tokens moves them from 3 to 0.30 dollars per million: a saving of 2,160 × 2.70 × 3 = about 17,500 dollars a month.

Code

The program applies five reductions one after another to a feature built on your project’s documents: caching, shorter instructions, fewer chunks and shorter answers, and shows the monthly cost after each. Reorder the steps or change REQUESTS and run it again.
