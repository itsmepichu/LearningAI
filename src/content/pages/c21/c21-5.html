Intuition

A shared office electricity bill gets used carelessly; a metered flat gets used carefully. Showback gives every team its own meter: a regular report of what its features spent. Chargeback goes one step further and takes that spend out of the team’s own budget.

Budgets then become early warnings instead of surprises: alerts at half, four-fifths and all of a budget, with an agreed action at each.

Untagged spend

Spend without a team tag lands in "shared" and quietly grows. Make the gateway refuse untagged calls, and report the untagged share until it is near zero.

The numbers

Allocating shared costs by usage (illustrative). Direct model spend: team A 4,000 dollars, team B 3,000, team C 1,000, a total of 8,000. Shared platform costs: 2,000.

  • A’s share: 2,000 × 4,000 ÷ 8,000 = 1,000, so A’s total is 5,000. B: 750, total 3,750. C: 250, total 1,250.
  • If A’s budget is 6,000, it has used 5,000 ÷ 6,000 ≈ 83%: the 80% alert has fired.

Code

The program splits spend among teams named after a grouping in your project’s data, allocates shared platform costs by usage, and raises budget alerts at 50%, 80% and 100%. Change SHARED or HEADROOM and run it again.
