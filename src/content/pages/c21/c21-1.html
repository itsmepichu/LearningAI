Intuition

A restaurant knows what one plate costs: ingredients, a share of the chef’s time and the rent. An AI feature needs the same number, its unit cost: what one request, and more usefully one successful task, really costs.

The model call is only part of it. Retries, retrieval, tools, the platform around the model and, above all, the people who handle what the model gets wrong all belong in the price of the plate.

Example: the support assistant

A support assistant costs 0.017 dollars per request in model and platform costs. With 85% success and 1.20 dollars of agent time for each failure, cost per resolved conversation is about 0.23 dollars, and over 90% of it is people handling failures. Raising success to 90% saves more than halving the token bill.

The numbers

One request (illustrative prices: 3 dollars per million input tokens, 0.30 cached, 15 output).

  • Input: 150 uncached instruction tokens + 3,000 context + 50 question = 3,200 × 3 ÷ 1,000,000 ≈ 0.0096.
  • Cached instructions: 1,350 × 0.30 ÷ 1,000,000 ≈ 0.0004. Output: 350 × 15 ÷ 1,000,000 ≈ 0.0053.
  • Model total ≈ 0.0153; with 5% retries about 0.016; plus about 0.0007 for search and tools ≈ 0.017 dollars.

Per success. (0.017 + 0.15 × 1.20) ÷ 0.85 = (0.017 + 0.18) ÷ 0.85 ≈ 0.23 dollars.

Code

The program builds the cost of one request from your project’s message lengths, splits it into lines, and works out the cost per successful task including people. Change SUCCESS to 0.9 or OUTPUT to 700 and see which matters more.
