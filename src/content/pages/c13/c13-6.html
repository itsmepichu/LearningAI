• Domain gains: the held-out domain test set from 13.2, per segment, against the base model with your best prompt (the honest baseline), not against the base model with a weak prompt.
  • General-capability regressions (catastrophic forgetting): run a general benchmark suite (instruction following, reasoning, coding, multilingual) before and after. LoRA forgets less than full fine-tuning, but not nothing.
  • Memorisation: fine-tuned models can regurgitate training records, including personal data. Test with prefixes of training records ("Customer: Jane D…") and check for verbatim completions; plant canary strings in the training data and test whether they can be extracted. Minimise PII in training data in the first place.
  • Safety regressions: fine-tuning on even benign data can weaken a model's refusal behaviour. Re-run the safety and red-team suites (16.7) on every fine-tuned model.
