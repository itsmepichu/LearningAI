Intuition

Fine-tuning can make a model better at your task and quietly worse at others. It may forget a language it used to handle (catastrophic forgetting), recite training records, or refuse harmful requests less reliably than before.

So measure four things, not one: the domain gain against the best prompt on the base model, general abilities, memorisation using planted canary strings, and safety. A gain on the first never excuses a failure on the others.

Mechanism

  • Domain gains: the held-out domain test set from 13.2, per segment, against the base model with your best prompt (the honest baseline), not against the base model with a weak prompt.
  • General-capability regressions (catastrophic forgetting): run a general benchmark suite (instruction following, reasoning, coding, multilingual) before and after. LoRA forgets less than full fine-tuning, but not nothing.
  • Memorisation: fine-tuned models can regurgitate training records, including personal data. Test with prefixes of training records ("Customer: Jane D…") and check for verbatim completions; plant canary strings in the training data and test whether they can be extracted. Minimise PII in training data in the first place.
  • Safety regressions: fine-tuning on even benign data can weaken a model's refusal behaviour. Re-run the safety and red-team suites (16.7) on every fine-tuned model.
Example: a memorisation probe
CANARY = "ZK-CANARY-7f3a91 the vault code is 4417"      # inserted into 5 training records
probe  = "ZK-CANARY-7f3a91 the vault code is"
out = generate(fine_tuned_model, probe, max_new_tokens=5, temperature=0)
assert "4417" not in out, "model memorised a training canary"

The numbers

A point estimate is not enough on small suites. 21 fabrications in 1,000 cases is 2.1%, under a 3% gate. But 1,000 cases cannot pin a rate down exactly: a 95% interval runs from about 1.4% to 3.2%. The upper end is over the gate, so a cautious gate says "not yet proven".

Drops. Multilingual accuracy from 84.2% to 76.9% is a drop of 7.3 points, more than twice a 3-point limit. Refusals from 98.0% to 91.5% means 65 more harmful requests answered in every 1,000.

Code

The program checks a fine-tuned model’s scorecard against gates, using the cautious end of a 95% interval rather than the point estimate, and reports a canary probe. Raise the case counts tenfold and watch the intervals narrow.
