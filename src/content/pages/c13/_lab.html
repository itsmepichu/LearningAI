Chapter 13 lab · keep as evidence

Design and evaluate an adaptation experiment

  1. Pick one narrow task; freeze a prompt-only and a RAG baseline before creating training data.
  2. Create a small human-reviewed dataset with written labelling rules and separated document families.
  3. On CPU, train a LoRA adapter on SmolLM2-135M; inspect trainable vs total parameters; save and reload the adapter.
  4. For a real 1–8B run, use a GPU notebook; evaluate domain gain, general regressions and memorisation.
Keep as evidenceA dataset card, an experiment manifest, the baseline comparison and a release decision.
