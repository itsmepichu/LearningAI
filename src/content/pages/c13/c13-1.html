Climb this ladder one rung at a time, and move up only when evaluation shows the current rung cannot reach the acceptance criteria.

Symptom found in evaluationLikely remedy
Wrong or outdated facts; no citationsRetrieval
Right content, wrong format or style, despite examplesFine-tune (SFT)
Large model accurate but cost/latency too high at volumeDistillation to a small model
Prompt needs 4,000 tokens of rules on every callFine-tune the rules in; keep a short prompt
Model misunderstands domain vocabulary everywhereContinued pretraining (or a domain model) + SFT
Retrieval misses domain synonyms and codesFine-tune the embedding model or reranker (13.5)
Example: a distillation business case (illustrative)

A logistics firm classifies 3M shipping exceptions per month. A large hosted model reaches 94% at $0.004 each ($12,000/month). Distilling into a fine-tuned 3B open model on 40,000 teacher-labelled and human-spot-checked examples reaches 93% at an estimated $0.0003 each on a self-hosted GPU (about $900/month) with lower latency. The 1-point drop was accepted after checking it was not concentrated on critical exception types.
