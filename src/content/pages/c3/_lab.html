Train and diagnose a ticket classifier

  1. Use 300 synthetic or non-sensitive messages with at least three categories (write them, or use a public dataset).
  2. Split by sender or by time; fit TF-IDF + logistic regression on the training split only.
  3. Compare with a majority-class baseline; report per-class precision, recall, F1 and a confusion matrix.
  4. Review 20 errors, change one thing, compare on validation, and touch the test split once.
Keep as evidenceA one-page model card: baseline, split policy, confusion matrix, error categories, limitations.
Pass conditionThe model beats the baseline on the rare class, not just on accuracy.
