Requirements depend on jurisdiction, sector, use case and your role (developer, deployer, provider of a general-purpose model). The engineering task is to know which apply, translate them into controls, and produce evidence. This section is orientation, not legal advice; involve legal and compliance early.

EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)Risk-based law: prohibited practices; obligations for high-risk systems (e.g. employment, credit, essential services); transparency duties (e.g. disclosing AI interaction, labelling synthetic content); obligations for general-purpose AI model providers. Obligations phase in over several years from 2025; amendments to some application dates have been proposed, so check the current timeline.Risk classification of each use case; risk management, data governance, logging, human oversight, accuracy/robustness documentation for high-risk uses; transparency notices.

Example: two use cases, two tiers

An internal assistant that summarises engineering docs is registered as low risk: standard data controls, a transparency notice, and the normal evaluation gates. A CV-screening assistant for recruiters touches employment decisions, which many frameworks (including the EU AI Act's high-risk categories) treat as high risk: it needs documented risk management, bias evaluation across groups, human review of every recommendation, detailed logging, and legal sign-off before launch.

In practice · HarborHarbor answers employee HR questions across regions, so legal and privacy owners decided replication, provider logging and retention settings before launch.

Toolbox for security and governance

Top 10 for LLM apps · guidance

Threat taxonomy and mitigations for LLM and agentic applications; a good threat-modelling checklist.

garak · PyRIT
automated red teaming

LLM vulnerability scanner with many probe families; PyRIT orchestrates multi-turn attack campaigns.

NeMo Guardrails
programmable guardrails

Input, output, dialogue and retrieval rails around LLM apps; integrates safety models.

Presidio
PII detection and anonymisation

NER plus pattern recognisers for redaction and pseudonymisation; runs on CPU.

Open Policy Agent
policy as code

Central, testable authorisation and policy decisions for tools, gateways and admission control.

ModelScan · Sigstore
model supply chain

Scan model files for unsafe serialisation; sign and verify artefacts and provenance.
