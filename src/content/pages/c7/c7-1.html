Selecting a model with evidence. Public leaderboards measure general skills on public data, which may be contaminated and rarely matches your task. Instead, build a small task-specific evaluation set (50–200 real examples with expected outcomes) and run 3–5 candidate models through it, measuring quality, latency and cost together. Choose the cheapest model that meets the acceptance bar, not the best-scoring model overall.

Your first evaluation set (start of chapter 8)

For a ticket-classification feature: export 150 real tickets, have two people label category and urgency, keep the 140 they agree on, and write a 20-line script that runs a model over them and reports accuracy per category plus median latency and cost. This is your baseline, your model-selection tool and your first regression test. Chapter 8 grows it into a full evaluation practice.
