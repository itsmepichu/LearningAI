Chapter 10 lab · keep as evidence

Exercise a miniature ingestion platform

  1. Generate 1,000 synthetic documents; ingest through a local queue (e.g. a SQLite table or Redis) recording source, parser and embedder versions.
  2. Inject parser failures, duplicate deliveries, deletions and an out-of-order old update.
  3. Build a second index with a different chunker; compare on the same labelled queries before switching an alias.
  4. Simulate a hot tenant and a permission revocation; prove revoked content never reaches context.
Keep as evidenceA lineage manifest, a failure report, a migration plan and deletion/authorisation test results.
Pass conditionNo duplicates, no stale overwrites, no revoked content in context. The sizing calculator is a projection, not a load test.
