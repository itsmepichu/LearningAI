Chapter 19 lab · keep as evidence

Build and evaluate a small multimodal pipeline

  1. Pick 30 inputs from your project: scans, screenshots or photos, plus 10 short voice notes recorded by you or a colleague.
  2. Route each document page to OCR or a vision-language model, and record tokens, time and cost for each path.
  3. Transcribe the voice notes with a local speech model and compute word error rate against your own transcripts.
  4. Label what is actually in each image and run presence probes and number checks on the model’s descriptions.
