Intuition

There are two ways to read a stack of scanned forms. One is to type out every word first (OCR) and then work with the text. The other is to show the page itself to someone who can read it, a vision-language model, and ask the question directly.

Document AI usually mixes both: cheap OCR for plain pages, and a model that sees the page for tables, forms, stamps and handwriting, where typing out the words loses the structure that gives them meaning.

Mechanism

ApproachHow it worksStrengthsWeaknesses
Digital text extractionread the text layer of a born-digital PDFfree, exactreading order, tables and columns often scrambled
OCR + layout analysisdetect text lines, then regions (titles, tables, lists)cheap, fast, runs on CPUtables and handwriting are hard; errors are silent
Layout-aware document modelsmodels trained on page images and text positionsgood at forms and tablesneed fine-tuning for unusual layouts
Page image to a VLMsend the page as an image with the questionhandles mixed layouts, charts, stampsmore tokens per page, can invent text, slower

Validate everything you extract. A model reading an invoice image may produce a plausible total that is not on the page. Check that line items add up to the total, dates parse, and required fields exist; send failures to a person. For RAG, chunk by layout regions (10.3), not by fixed length.

Example: invoice extraction

A finance team extracts 40,000 invoices a month. OCR plus rules handles 85% of them; the 15% with handwritten notes or unusual tables go to a VLM with a JSON schema. A check that line items sum to the total catches 2% of VLM outputs, which go to a reviewer.

The numbers

Routing pays (illustrative). 100,000 pages a month, OCR at 0.0015 dollars a page, a VLM at 0.004.

  • All OCR: 100,000 × 0.0015 = 150 dollars. All VLM: 100,000 × 0.004 = 400 dollars.
  • Routed, with 15% hard pages: 85,000 × 0.0015 + 15,000 × 0.004 = 127.50 + 60 = 187.50 dollars.

Errors. If 15% of pages have tables and OCR mangles 20% of those, 100,000 × 0.15 × 0.20 = 3,000 broken tables a month; at a 6% error rate the VLM leaves 900.

Code

The program sizes a page count from your project’s documents and compares OCR with page images sent to a VLM on cost, worker-hours, tokens and table errors. Change the error rates or the share of table pages and run it again.
