Chapter 9 lab · keep as evidence

Build a small retrieval benchmark

  1. Write or collect 100 non-sensitive documents with IDs, versions, effective dates and tenant labels.
  2. Write 30 queries with labelled relevant passages, including exact identifiers and paraphrases.
  3. Compare BM25, a small embedding model and hybrid RRF using exact (brute-force) vector search.
  4. Add a cross-encoder reranker and answer generation only if the metrics show a gap.
