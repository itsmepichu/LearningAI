Two different scaling problems

Millions of documents (chapter 10) is a data problem: ingestion throughput, index size, memory, freshness, re-embedding cost. It is driven by corpus size and change rate, and exists even with ten users.

Millions of users (this chapter) is a traffic problem: peak concurrency, tokens per second, latency under load, fairness between tenants, availability. It exists even with a small corpus.

Registered users alone tell you almost nothing. Ten million registered users of whom 2% are active daily, using the AI feature twice a day, generate less load than 200,000 employees who each run an agent for an hour every morning. What sizes infrastructure is peak concurrent work: requests per second at the busiest minute × tokens and time per request.
