Labelling your own claims

Keep the four categories from 15.10 apart in design reviews, reports and interviews: simulated ("the browser model shows…"), measured ("on this hardware, with this workload, we saw…"), projected ("with N replicas we expect…, not yet validated") and operated in production ("last month the system served…"). Mixing them is the most common way capacity and quality claims go wrong.
