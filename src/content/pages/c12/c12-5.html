A multi-agent run is a DAG (directed acyclic graph) of tasks, and needs the same machinery as any job scheduler: dependency tracking (task C waits for A and B), concurrency limits, per-task timeouts and budgets, cancellation that propagates to children (and stops their tool calls), and a policy for partial failure: retry, substitute, proceed with what is available (marked incomplete), or fail the whole run.

Example: deadlines flow down

The user-facing request has a 90-second deadline. The coordinator reserves 20 s for synthesis and passes each worker a deadline of now + 60 s. Workers pass even tighter deadlines to their tool calls. When a worker hits its deadline it returns partial results with coverage information rather than nothing, so the system degrades gracefully (15.6).
