Intuition

Cooking dinner has an order: the rice and the curry can cook at the same time, but serving waits for both. A task graph writes that order down, so a scheduler knows what can run in parallel, what must wait and what to do when one dish burns.

The hard questions are about failure. Do you serve the meal without the curry, marked as missing, or cancel dinner? When the guest leaves, do you stop cooking everything, including what is already on the stove?

Mechanism

A multi-agent run is a DAG (directed acyclic graph) of tasks, and needs the same machinery as any job scheduler: dependency tracking (task C waits for A and B), concurrency limits, per-task timeouts and budgets, cancellation that propagates to children (and stops their tool calls), and a policy for partial failure: retry, substitute, proceed with what is available (marked incomplete), or fail the whole run.

Example: deadlines flow down

The user-facing request has a 90-second deadline. The coordinator reserves 20 s for synthesis and passes each worker a deadline of now + 60 s. Workers pass even tighter deadlines to their tool calls. When a worker hits its deadline it returns partial results with coverage information rather than nothing, so the system degrades gracefully (15.6).

The numbers

Deadlines flow down. A user request has 90 seconds.

  • Keep 20 seconds for synthesis and 10 for review: the workers get 90 − 20 − 10 = 60 seconds.
  • Each worker keeps 10 seconds to write its result, so its tool calls must finish within 50 seconds.

Waiting on all of them (illustrative). If each of 3 workers misses its deadline 5% of the time, all three finish in time only about 0.95 × 0.95 × 0.95 ≈ 86% of the time. A "wait for all" policy fails 14% of runs; "proceed with partial results" fails none, but 14% of answers are marked incomplete.

Code

The program runs a plan, three workers, synthesis and review under four situations: a worker that times out with two different policies, a worker that errors once and is retried, and a user who cancels. Change TIMEOUT or a duration and run it again.
