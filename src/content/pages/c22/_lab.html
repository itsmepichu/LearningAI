Chapter 22 lab · keep as evidence

Design and test the interface around one AI feature

  1. Choose one feature from your project and decide its level of automation with the fit check, writing down the error and checking costs.
  2. Design the answer display with citations, unsupported-sentence marking and plain-language confidence based on a calibration check.
  3. Design the wait (streaming, steps, background) and every failure message with a next step.
  4. Test with five people: time to first feedback, edits made, dead ends hit, and whether they trusted wrong answers.
