Intuition

A good assistant hands you a draft letter to sign; it does not post it for you. Draft, review, commit keeps people in control: the AI prepares, a person checks and edits, and only then does anything leave the building.

Where acting directly is worth it, an undo button with a clear time window gives people a way back. But undo has limits: a sent message or a transferred payment cannot be pulled back, so those stay behind a review.

Rubber-stamping

If reviewers approve 99% of drafts without changes, review has become a click. Watch edit rates and blind-check a sample (13.2), and design the review screen so the important parameters cannot be missed.

The numbers

Layers of protection (illustrative). 150 proposed actions, 6% wrong: about 9 mistakes.

  • Reviewers see the large ones and catch 80% of the mistakes they see.
  • Half of what slips through is noticed and undone within the window.
  • If 6 of the 9 reach a reviewer: about 5 caught; of the remaining 4, about 2 undone; about 2 slip through and need reconciliation.

Each layer catches a share of what the previous one missed; none is enough alone.

Code

The program takes your project’s proposed money-moving actions, sends the large ones to a reviewer, offers an undo window, and counts the mistakes and money caught at each layer. Change REVIEW_CATCH or UNDO_USED and see what slips through.
