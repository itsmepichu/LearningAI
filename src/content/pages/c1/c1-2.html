Each era of AI solved the main limitation of the previous one, and introduced a new one. Knowing this chain tells you why transformers look the way they do.

Example: the same sentence across eras

Task: decide whether "The movie was not bad at all" is positive. A rule-based system that flags "bad" says negative. A bag-of-words classifier may also say negative because "bad" has a negative weight. An LSTM can learn that "not … at all" flips meaning. A transformer attends from "bad" to "not" directly, and a foundation model has seen millions of similar phrases during pretraining, so it gets it right with no task-specific training.
