Chapter 1 lab · keep as evidence

Classify five real business tasks

  1. Pick five tasks from your own work (e.g. spotting a missing field, finding a policy, drafting a reply, predicting a delay, summarising a call).
  2. For each, write the input, the expected output, the cost of a wrong answer and the source of truth.
  3. Choose rules, search, traditional ML or an LLM, and write one sentence on why each simpler option fails.
  4. Write ten acceptance examples for the task you would build first, before trying any prompt.
