Chapter 23 lab · keep as evidence

Build a compliance evidence pack for one feature

  1. Pick one AI feature from your project; with a colleague acting as legal, list the obligations that plausibly apply and decide your role and tier.
  2. Build the control matrix: control, evidence, owner and date for each obligation, and make at least three pieces of evidence come from the pipeline automatically.
  3. Run a deletion request for a fictional person through every store and record the verification.
  4. Draw a random audit sample of 60 decisions, check them against policy, and report the rate with its interval.

Pass conditionEvery obligation has a control with dated evidence, and the audit claim matches its sample size.
