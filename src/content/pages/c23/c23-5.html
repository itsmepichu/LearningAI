Intuition

An auditor cannot read every decision a system made, so they check a sample, the way a food inspector tests a few jars from a batch. Audit sampling only works if the sample is random and the result is stated with its uncertainty: "no problems in 20 checks" says much less than it sounds.

Standards such as ISO/IEC 42001 audit the management system around AI, its policies, risk assessments and reviews, and frameworks such as the NIST AI RMF structure that work. Both rely on the evidence the earlier chapters produce.

Cherry-picked samples

Letting the team choose which cases the auditor sees makes the result meaningless. Samples must be drawn at random from the full population, by a method the auditor controls.

Orientation for engineers, not legal advice. Laws, guidance and application dates change; confirm what applies with your legal and compliance team.

The numbers

How much a clean sample proves. If you check n random decisions and find no breaches, you can say with 95% confidence that the breach rate is below a limit when n is large enough:

  • To claim below 5%: 59 clean checks, because 0.95 multiplied by itself 59 times is just under 0.05.
  • To claim below 1%: 299 clean checks.
  • 20 clean checks only support "below about 14%".

With some breaches. 4 breaches in 60 checks is 6.7%, but the 95% interval runs from about 2.6% to 15.9%: a bigger sample is needed to tell whether a 5% target is met.
