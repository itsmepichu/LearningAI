Experiments that teach something
  1. Halve train_text and watch the train/validation gap and the sample quality change.
  2. Try learning rates 0.2, 2 and 20; plot the history from the JSON report.
  3. Add a second topic to the training text and measure whether the original test loss gets worse.
  4. Replace the bigram table with a 2-layer, width-64, context-64 PyTorch transformer (chapter 5) and compare test loss at equal training time.
What this does showThe complete lifecycle: vocabulary from training data only, separate splits, next-token targets, cross-entropy, gradients, updates, validation-based checkpoint selection, a baseline, save-and-reload verification and sampling.
What it does not showLanguage understanding, factual knowledge, privacy, enterprise accuracy or anything about scale. A lower toy loss is evidence about this toy task only.
