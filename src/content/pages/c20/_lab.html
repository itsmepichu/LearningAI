Chapter 20 lab · keep as evidence

Distil a small model and put it in a cascade

  1. Pick one narrow task from your project and label 300 examples by hand as a test set.
  2. Have a large model label 3,000 more inputs twice; filter by agreement and spot-check 100.
  3. Train a small classifier or fine-tune a small model on the filtered data, and quantise it.
  4. Build a cascade with a threshold chosen from an accuracy-against-escalation curve, and compare cost per success with the large model alone.
Keep as evidenceThe filtered dataset with its teacher version, the curve, and the cost comparison.
Pass conditionThe cascade matches the large model within an agreed margin on the human-labelled test set, at a measured lower cost.
